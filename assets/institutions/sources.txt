TacClaw-Infinity: institution logo sources
Retrieved: 2026-10-09

The affiliation numbers match sections/00_frontmatter.tex.
SVG artwork is retained beside PNG renderings where available. Existing vector paths were used for icon-only variants; wordmark paths were removed where requested. Legacy white raster backgrounds are composited away in the PDF with multiply blending; those source PNGs may still contain white pixels.
Daimon uses its original vector silhouette in the official favicon red (#E61736), without a surrounding tile.
All 16 institution positions are filled and arranged on one row. No number labels are displayed below the logos.
